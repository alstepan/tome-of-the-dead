TOME OF THE DEAD

Big-character, late-80s arcade-adventure look: tall 3×6-cell (24×48) heroes, one INK per sprite on BLACK PAPER, all detail cut in with negative (paper) lines instead of extra colours. Every frame is 1-bit and padded to whole 8-px cells so it drops straight into a pre-shifted or masked sprite routine. Faint lines mark attribute-cell boundaries.

IN-GAME MOCK

SCREEN BUDGET

Rows 0–159: side-on room (diorama view). Rows 160–191: HUD with health pips, mana pips and a 4-slot inventory strip.

Room scenery uses dim INK 1 / INK 2 so bright sprites pop; sprites take their cell's attribute as they move (colour follows the character, background tiles recolour underneath).

Memory: a 24×48 frame is 144 B, 288 B masked. The knight’s 11 frames ≈ 2 KB unmasked; everything on this sheet fits comfortably in one 16 KB 128K bank, even with masks.

To avoid clash entirely, switch the room to a single INK (monochrome play area) and keep colour for the HUD only.
