# Devlog post (type: Major Update or Launch)

**Title:** Tome of the Dead v1.2: bigger depths, keys, new monsters and a tutorial

**Tome of the Dead v1.2 is out.** This is the biggest update so far.

**What's new**

- **Bigger depths.** Each depth now has 10 to 12 rooms instead of 9. A game lasts longer.
- **Keys and locked doors.** Keys come in two colours. Find the right key to open the way on.
- **Three new monsters.**
  - The **Gargoyle** looks like a statue until you come close.
  - The **Fire Imp** spits fire.
  - The **Ice Shade** (Frost Wraith) can freeze you.
  - A new amulet, the Frost Charm, protects you from cold.
- **A tutorial.** Brother Morrow shows you how to play on a short first depth, then you go straight into the game.
- **Item details.** Pick an item in the bag to see what it does.
- **Smarter monsters.** They follow you further and come back to where they were.
- **More things in the rooms**, and an **XP bar** on the screen.

**Smaller changes**

- Slower hunger.
- A safer Teleport.
- Stronger monsters deeper down.
- Mage spell tuning.
- A message when you are too weak to jump.
- A flash on a new ability icon.

**How to play**

Play in your browser on this page, or download the `.tap` or `.sna` for an emulator or a real Spectrum 128K. It does not run on a 48K.

There may still be bugs. Please tell me about them in the comments, and share how your games go. Thanks for playing!
